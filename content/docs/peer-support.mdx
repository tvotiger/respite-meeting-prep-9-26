**Highlight 2 of 6** from the Sept 17 Commission worksheets.

## What happened

Peer and support-type services drew specific scrutiny for how fast use and spending grew:

- <Term id="utilization">Utilization</Term> rose from **9.25 hours per person per year** in FY2022–23 to **24.75** in FY2025–26.
- One service code increased from **$8.8 million** to **$51.8 million**.

<Term id="hcpf">HCPF</Term>’s review found these services were being delivered **without adequate <Term id="clinical-oversight">clinical oversight</Term>** — meaning not enough professional review of whether delivery was appropriate and controlled.

## Plain English

People were using many more hours of these supports, one billing category roughly **sextupled** in spend, and the state thinks supervision / oversight did not keep up.

## Why a newcomer should care

This is the “growth without guardrails” story. It feeds themes of <Term id="program-integrity">program integrity</Term>, earlier problem detection, and real-time technology flags — not only rate cuts.
