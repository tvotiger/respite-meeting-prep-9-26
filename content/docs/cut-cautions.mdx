## What HCPF cautioned

<Term id="hcpf">HCPF</Term> cautioned that:

1. Expenditure figures are **approximate ranges** — not precise estimates of savings from repealing legislation.
2. Some reductions can **move costs into more expensive settings** rather than produce true savings (<Term id="cost-shifting">cost shifting</Term> — e.g. less home support → more crisis / institutional care).
3. <Term id="federal-match">Federal matching</Term> rates mean apparent cuts produce very different <Term id="general-fund">General Fund</Term> savings depending on which population is affected.

## Plain English

“Cut $X of <Term id="medicaid">Medicaid</Term> services” does **not** automatically mean “save $X for Colorado.” The federal share, who is cut, and where people go next all change the math — sometimes cutting home care can cost the state more later.

## Why a newcomer should care

This is the official caution providers lean on: do not treat service reduction as the first or only lever. Fix complexity, oversight, and design first where possible.
