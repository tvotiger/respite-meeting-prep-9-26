**Highlight 4 of 6** from the Sept 17 Commission worksheets.

## What commissioners were asked

The worksheet asked whether more <Term id="medicaid">Medicaid</Term> dollars are going to **administration, <Term id="case-management">case management</Term>, contracts, and vendors** rather than **direct service** (the actual help a member receives).

## Important caution

<Term id="hcpf">HCPF</Term> materials did **not** identify this admin / middle layer as a driver of the spending growth being discussed. It was put on the table as a question — not as HCPF’s official explanation of the $1.26B growth.

## One number from the worksheet

Case management spending was listed at **$161.2 million** in FY2025–26 across **96,259** members.

Case management = coordinating plans and authorizations, not necessarily the person doing laundry or personal care. See [Concepts to know first](/docs/concepts-first).

## Why a newcomer should care

This is an <Term id="administrative-sustainability">administrative sustainability</Term> opening: even if admin was not blamed as *the* growth driver, simplifying paperwork and duplication can still free dollars for care.
