**Highlight 3 of 6** from the Sept 17 Commission worksheets.

## What happened

<Term id="hcpf">HCPF</Term> materials said the original <Term id="cfc">CFC</Term> forecast “did not sufficiently align policy and fiscal assumptions.”

The forecast also did **not** anticipate how providers would respond — creating a significant gap between projected and actual spending.

## Plain English

When Colorado designed CFC, the budget math and the real-world program rules did not match well enough. Planners also underestimated how agencies and families would actually use the new flexibility. Result: surprise costs.

## Why a newcomer should care

This is why providers argue for **operational input before design changes** — people who run services day to day often see how <Term id="utilization">utilization</Term> will behave before a spreadsheet does.
