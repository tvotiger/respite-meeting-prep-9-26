6. Budget-cutting cautions

Why apparent Medicaid cuts often do not save what people think

Highlight 6 of 6 from the Sept 17 Commission worksheets.

What HCPF cautioned

cautioned that:

  1. Expenditure figures are approximate ranges — not precise estimates of savings from repealing legislation.
  2. Some reductions can move costs into more expensive settings rather than produce true savings ( — e.g. less home support → more crisis / institutional care).
  3. rates mean apparent cuts produce very different savings depending on which population is affected.

Plain English

“Cut $X of services” does not automatically mean “save $X for Colorado.” The federal share, who is cut, and where people go next all change the math — sometimes cutting home care can cost the state more later.

Why a newcomer should care

This is the official caution providers lean on: do not treat service reduction as the first or only lever. Fix complexity, oversight, and design first where possible.
